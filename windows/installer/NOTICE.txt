Headroom for Windows
====================

Headroom boosts the volume of everything your PC plays: browsers, Spotify,
games, video players, calls and system sounds. A built-in limiter keeps the
louder sound from clipping or distorting.

How it works
------------
Windows doesn't let normal apps change other apps' sound. Headroom therefore
uses Equalizer APO, a free, open-source audio engine that plugs into Windows'
own audio processing. Headroom's limiter runs inside that engine, so the boost
applies to every sound, all the time, even when the Headroom window is closed.

During setup, Equalizer APO asks which audio devices to use. Tick the
speakers and headphones you listen on.

Privacy
-------
All audio is processed locally on your PC. Headroom has no servers, no
analytics and no accounts. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

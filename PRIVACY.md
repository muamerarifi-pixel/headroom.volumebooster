# Headroom: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 27, 2026_

Headroom is a browser extension that increases the volume of audio playing in browser tabs.

**No data collection.** Headroom does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data. It has no servers, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party services.

**Audio.** Tab audio is processed entirely on your device, inside your browser, to apply volume gain, auto-leveling, voice clarity, and limiting. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

**Settings.** Your preferences (boost level, limiter style, Auto-Level and Voice Clarity toggles, on/off state, per-site pinned profiles, and websites where you turned boosting off) are saved locally with the browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local` / `chrome.storage.session`) on your own device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

**Permissions.** Headroom asks for access to websites only so it can route each page's audio and video players through its local Web Audio limiter. It does not read, collect, or modify any other page content.

**Contact.** Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/muamerarifi-pixel/headroom.volumebooster/issues
